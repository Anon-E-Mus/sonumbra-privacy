Sonumbra

Privacy Policy - effective November 2026

Sonumbra is a tinnitus relief companion app. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: almost everything stays on your device, and what little leaves it is anonymous by default.

What stays on your device

Your tinnitus profile, daily check-ins, session progress, streaks, spike log, reflections, theme choice and all settings are stored only in the app's local database on your phone. They are never uploaded unless you explicitly turn on cross-device sync, and even then they are tied to an anonymous identifier - not your name, email or phone number.

What we collect and why

Anonymous account. When you finish the free tinnitus check, the app creates an anonymous, random-looking identifier so your program access works across reinstalls. It contains no personal information. If you never buy anything, it stays anonymous forever.

Purchases. Subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play. We receive a notification that a purchase happened (to unlock your access) - we never see your card, your payment details, or your Google account.

Community posts. If you post in the in-app community, your posts are stored under a randomly generated pseudonym (like "Quiet Otter #4821"). We never ask for or display real names.

Tester feedback. If you join the founding tester program, your daily feedback (ratings, sound preferences, a note you choose to write) is stored to improve the app.

Usage statistics

To make Sonumbra better, the app collects anonymous, aggregated usage statistics - which features are opened, which sounds are used and for how long, where people get stuck in onboarding, and how far testers and members progress through the program. These statistics are tied only to your anonymous app identifier, are analyzed in aggregate, and are never used to build advertising profiles or shared with anyone.

We also record anonymous engagement signals (like streak breaks or returns after a break) so we can understand what keeps members benefiting - and reach out with help, not hype, when someone drifts away.

Email you give us

If you choose to share your email (for founding-member updates, your outcomes report, or early access to new tools), we use it only to send you those things. You can unsubscribe with one tap at any time and your email is then deleted. We never sell, rent or share email addresses, and we never add you to a list you did not explicitly ask for.

What we never do

  • No ads, ever - so no ad trackers, no advertising identifiers, no third-party profiling.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos or microphone audio.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs that fingerprint your device.

Account deletion

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To also remove the anonymous server record (profile backup, posts, feedback), contact support@sonumbra.app or use "Delete my data" in the app's settings. Deletion is final and immediate.

Children

Sonumbra is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Health information disclaimer

Sonumbra is a wellness app, not a medical device, and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Nothing in the app is medical advice. If your tinnitus began suddenly, pulses with your heartbeat, or occurs with hearing loss or dizziness, please see a doctor promptly.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the app will tell you before the change takes effect.
